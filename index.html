THE CORE

HOW IT WORKS

01

LAUNCH

Play a project from your hand. The launch MC lands on your market cap right away, and the project takes a position in your portfolio.

02

PUMP

At the end of your turn every position in your portfolio adds its pump to your MC. Six positions, so opening one means closing another.

03

RUG

Snipe, FUD and Rug Pull strip holders off their projects. At zero the project rugs — off the table, and the pump stops for good.
